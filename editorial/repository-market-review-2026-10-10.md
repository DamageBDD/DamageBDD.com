# Repository-to-market review — 10 October 2026

## Outcome and scope

This pack contains **13 long-form Org articles and one curated collection**, plus an insertion into the existing article index. It adds customer-problem entry points without replacing the implementation references or altering the publisher. The supplied website baseline contains 77 Org pages, including 39 article pages. Comparing that catalogue was separate from reviewing selected public implementation paths.

**This is not an exhaustive audit of the latest repository additions.** The complete GitHub tree, commit history and current HEAD could not be retrieved. Several readable branch pages were cached; repeated README fetches returned inconsistent snapshots. A current source archive or authenticated checkout is still needed to enumerate every addition and pin every claim to one revision. No latest commit SHA or release tag is fabricated here.

Readable runner, HTTP, application, supervisor, search-context, configuration and build paths provide direct evidence of selected mechanisms. The more recent private-index, repair, archive, signer and media descriptions rely on the supplied site's dated implementation references. Their historical source links are preserved as historical references, not relabelled current. The historical integration review includes material that was not present at its linked public revision.

No Erlang application tests, live APIs, payment flows, native workstation features or production deployments were executed. No Emacs/Org export was run. New page metadata records documentation review, not runtime validation; no new capability ID or verified-release claim is introduced.

## Lead with the buying problem

The recommended front door is **acceptance evidence for software and AI-assisted changes**. The buyer can provide a known failure, agree the expected behaviour and inspect a bounded result without first adopting every protocol in the stack. This is an editorial prioritisation, not measured product-market fit or a forecast of sales.

The [2025 Stack Overflow survey](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai) reports 46% distrust versus 33% trust in AI-tool accuracy. That supports investigation of a verification problem, not a claim that respondents would purchase DamageBDD. [DORA's 2025 overview](https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/) emphasises organisational conditions rather than treating an AI tool as an isolated productivity intervention. The series therefore proposes comparison with an existing workflow and asks for measured pilot outcomes.

Private knowledge is the next track: selected sources, explicit model destinations and inspectable answer provenance. [OWASP's disclosure guidance](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm022025-sensitive-information-disclosure/) supports least-privilege data access and restricted sources; it does not certify this implementation. The repair article keeps generated suggestions separate from privileged execution in line with [OWASP's excessive-agency guidance](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/).

Nostr signing, media and search should be specialist entry points for application builders with those needs already. Payment integration should describe a bounded job and reconciliation policy, not token liquidity or guaranteed earnings. erm belongs in a Linux/Erlang design-partner track until workstation-specific behaviour and recovery can be demonstrated.

| Specialist | Nostr publisher / client integrator | App needs limited signing authority | Actual-client permitted/refused signing and recovery demonstration | Policy outcomes; full request/reply; operator handoff | HSM custody or all-client coverage from one historical run |
| Specialist | Nostr / Bitcoin application team | Searchable records, durable media or paid jobs | Separate small interoperability and failure-handling pilots | Relevance; retrieval after restart; reconciliation effort | Current NIP-50 conformance, universal retention or instant liquidity without evidence |

These are offer designs, not advertised prices, proven demand or sales commitments. Start each proposal with a target/corpus the customer is authorised to use, written scope, named reviewer and a budget for failures. No target company, customer quote, revenue result or pricing number has been invented.

## Engineering and messaging checks before stronger claims

### Release and index identity

The [index NFT reference](../org/articles/ecai_index_nfts.org) and [7 October integration review](../org/articles/integration_status_2026_10_07.org) describe a dry-run mint helper and a job service whose paid flag is not settlement. Recheck current implementations before advertising mint submission, confirmation or automated job settlement. Do not conflate the index-NFT path with the separate release-discovery helper.

The same review records snapshot hash-verification and Wikidata-selection questions. These are historical unresolved findings, not results from a current re-run. A manifest, an index root and a provenance record identify particular claims; they do not establish relevance or semantic truth. [SLSA provenance](https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.2/provenance) remains a complementary standard, not a certification earned by attaching an NFT.

### Private knowledge and model access

The [private-knowledge reference](../org/articles/ecai_private_knowledge.org) describes decryption of scanned segments in trusted-worker memory, corpus-level permissions and specific model-destination controls. Do not write “the LLM operates on encrypted data” or imply row-level policy, complete remote-provider control or hallucination elimination. A no-source path and an unauthorised-source refusal need live validation, including the actual crypto backend.

### Nostr retention and protocol coverage

The [Nosternity reference](../org/articles/nosternity.org) describes a historical pending-queue durability gap. Admission, submission and confirmed storage are distinct. A later search implementation may improve some of these paths; its source was not available for this review, so no conclusion about those improvements is asserted. NIP-50 support must be checked against filters, ordering and client behaviour, not a search endpoint's name.

### Signer custody

The [nsecbunker reference](../org/articles/damage_nsecbunker.org) preserves a dated run with a specific release and scenario scope. Keep it historical. AWS Secrets Manager retrieval is not non-exportable HSM signing, and a current absence check is not proof of historical deletion. A new customer's full request/reply path and recovery procedure require their own evidence.

### Runner and repair recheck

In the readable cached runner, the concurrent path appeared to return spawned process identifiers while a caller expected map-shaped execution context. Treat this as a source-level question to reproduce against a pinned current tree, not a confirmed latest defect. It motivated the article's insistence on testing aggregation and failure reporting before using load results commercially. The readable HTTP snapshot explicitly marks TRACE unimplemented.

The [dated integration review](../org/articles/integration_status_2026_10_07.org) also records incident-learner publication and target-validation questions. The new repair article does not imply those findings have been closed or that reviewed test source means passing tests. A separate worktree is not an isolation boundary for untrusted code.

### Legacy marketing cleanup deliberately left separate

The existing [payments article](../org/articles/payments.org) contains strong immediate-liquidity, reserve and buyback language that the evidence examined here does not establish. The new payment guide does not repeat or link readers through those claims. Review that old article before promoting it alongside the new series. A footer disclaimer does not make an unsupported operational promise demonstrated.

Other older research, scale and truth claims should likewise be kept distinct from measured engineering results. This patch does not silently rewrite historical pages or upgrade their status. It creates a new buyer-facing route with narrower, demonstrable propositions.
